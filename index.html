Global Warming Is Accelerating

The Earth's temperature is rising faster than ever before. Extreme weather, melting ice caps, and ecosystem collapse are signals that urgent action is needed.

What Is Global Warming?

Global warming refers to the long-term increase in Earth’s average temperature caused by human activities, primarily fossil fuel consumption and deforestation.

Why It Matters

Rising sea levels, stronger hurricanes, droughts, and biodiversity loss endanger both human societies and natural ecosystems.

Key Effects

  • Melting polar ice and rising sea levels
  • Extreme heatwaves and wildfires
  • Ocean acidification
  • Loss of habitats and species extinction

Global Trends

Every decade, the Earth's average temperature increases. The concentration of atmospheric CO₂ is at its highest level in millions of years.
